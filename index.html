Who We Are

Three Decades of
Craftsmanship & Trust

Founded in Austin, Texas in 1993, High Tech Flooring & Design has grown from a local flooring specialist into one of the most trusted full-service flooring partners in the Texas market.

Over three decades, we have cultivated deep relationships with the state's most respected homebuilders and commercial developers — built on a foundation of quality, reliability, and design expertise that our partners have come to depend on.

From the design center to the final installation, our team delivers a seamless, white-glove experience that protects our builder partners' timelines, budgets, and reputations.

Design Excellence

Our design professionals help builders and homeowners navigate thousands of flooring options with confidence — from luxury vinyl plank to hardwood, tile, carpet, and beyond.

Unmatched Schedule Discipline

We actively track the production schedule of every single home in our pipeline — monitoring starts, stages, and milestones so materials arrive in our warehouse precisely when needed. No waiting. No delays. No surprises. It's a level of proactive coordination that sets us apart as a trade partner.

Scalable Capacity

From a single custom home to a 500-unit community, our infrastructure scales to meet demand across every segment we serve.

Trusted Partnership

Relationships are the cornerstone of our business. Our clients don't just use us — they grow with us, decade after decade.
